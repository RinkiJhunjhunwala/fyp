Mathematical Aggregation Procedure: At round $t$, the central server performs Federated Averaging (FedAvg): $$W_{t+1} = \sum_{k=1}^K \frac{n_k}{N} W_k^{(t)}, \quad b_{t+1} = \sum_{k=1}^K \frac{n_k}{N} b_k^{(t)}$$ where $n_k$ is the number of local training patients at hospital $k$, and $N = \sum_{k=1}^K n_k$ is the total decentralized training cohort size.
